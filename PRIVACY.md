# Privacy Policy — Speedometer No Ads

_Last updated: 2026-07-17_

Speedometer No Ads ("the app") is a GPS speedometer for golf carts, e-scooters, and e-bikes. Your
privacy is simple to explain: **the app does not collect, store, or share any personal
data.**

## Location

Speedometer No Ads uses your device's location (GPS) **only while the app is open and in the
foreground** to calculate your current speed, average speed, and trip distance.

- Your location is processed **on your device only**.
- It is **never** saved to a file, sent over the internet, or shared with us or any third
  party.
- When you close the app, location access stops.

## Data we collect

None. Speedometer No Ads has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no backend servers.

The only information stored is your **app preferences** (preferred units and selected
vehicle). These are saved locally on your device and are removed if you uninstall the app.

## Children's privacy

Speedometer No Ads does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
